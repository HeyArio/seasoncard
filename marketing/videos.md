# Short video scripts (Saman films, faceless, hands only)

Rules for every video: no faces, no real people's photos (rules.md). Hands, fabric/paper swatches by a window in daylight, or a screen recording of the scan on an illustration labelled "illustration". Say "might be", never "you are". Keep each video 15–25 seconds. Use TikTok's own music/text tools.

Caption template (every video): hook line, one comment question, `Want a personal colour review? Comment REVIEW 🎨`, `Free scan at seasoncard.app`, a plain-text search line, 4–5 hashtags. Mention "Season Club: comment your season" where it fits.

Rotation: drape test, "stop buying these colours if you're a ___", founder story, reply-to-comment (use once comments arrive). Add 3 new scripts every run.

### Video 1: Drape test (format: drape test)
**Props:** a plain white tee or sheet on a table by a window (daylight, no lamp), 2 fabric swatches or coloured paper sheets: a warm one (rust/peach) and a cool one (rose/berry). Your own hand only.

**Shot list**
1. 0–2s: Hand drops a warm swatch onto the white sheet, then a cool swatch next to it. Close-up, window light.
2. 2–6s: Hand lifts the warm swatch up toward the camera (the camera is your "face"), pauses.
3. 6–10s: Hand swaps to the cool swatch, same position.
